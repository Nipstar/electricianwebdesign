Questions from the trade

What electricians ask before committing

Do you understand electrical work?

Andy's background is field service, not electrical contracting, and he will not pretend otherwise. Over 30 years of it means he knows how reactive work runs. The technical content comes from what you supply: your service list, scheme numbers and photos.

Can you show my NICEIC or NAPIT badge?

Yes, your own, shown with your registration details once you confirm them. Antek Automation only displays a badge a client actually holds.

I get work from a lead site already. Why my own site?

Leads from a lead site are rented: you pay for them, and the customer may well be sent to other firms too. Enquiries from your own site are yours, and you keep the page that won them.

Can it take emergency calls out of hours?

Yes, with the AI receptionist, which is set up from £497 and runs from £97 a month. It answers in your business name day and night, takes the details and books or passes them on. AI voice agents for electricians covers it in depth.

How much does an electrician website cost?

Starter at £600 suits a sole trader with a tight list of jobs, and Core at £995 suits a firm with more to show. Extra job pages are £125, and hosting and care is £10 a month.

How long until my job pages are live?

Allow 1 to 3 weeks from your deposit. Scheme details and photos of real installs are normally what holds a build up, so have them to hand.

If I sell the business, does the website go with it?

You own the site once it is paid for. It can be passed on along with the rest of the firm's assets.

Do you build for other trades?

This site is only for electricians. Plumbers should look at plumberwebdesign.co.uk, and other trades at web design for tradespeople.
